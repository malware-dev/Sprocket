# Construct

A construct is a set of grids joined by mechanical blocks such as pistons, rotors and hinges. A ship with a rotor turret is one construct. Grids held by landing gear, connectors or magnetic plates are separate constructs, so a ship docked at a station is not part of the station.

The automation is kept in the construct's programmable blocks. Press `Automation` in the terminal of any of them to open the [home screen](#home), which lists every ![](mark:logic-system)&nbsp;[logic system](#logic-system) on the construct. It runs while any of them is working. With all of them switched off or without power it holds, and a ![](mark:sequence)&nbsp;[sequence](#sequence) part way through carries on from its step once one of them works again.

The automation belongs to the construct's owners, who alone can open it, and uses the blocks they may use. [Changing hands](#changing-hands) explains who the owners are, and what happens when the construct passes to another player.

A logic system can only use blocks on its own construct. A docked ship's blocks can't fill its [slots](#slot).

A construct needs a programmable block to keep its automation. When its last one is destroyed or ground down, the logic systems the [library](#library) doesn't already have are kept there, as backups.
