# The language spec

`docs/Language.md`. Its reader is somebody writing or reading a script.

- It holds what the language is now: shape, values, constructs, an example of each.
- No history, rationale, rejected alternatives, roadmap or undecided points.
- The old wording is deleted, never kept beside the new.
- Only the language. How Sprocket or an editor behaves is not a rule of the language.
- A parser limit is not a rule. What is unsupported because nothing needs it yet is not mentioned.
- It says what the language is. An absence is stated only where a reader from another language would
  assume the opposite ("no continuations" is; "no comma between parts" is not).
- Writing conventions are shown in the syntax and examples, beside the thing they are about, never in
  a section of their own and never as an obligation.
- Every sentence carries something. A lead-in or a heading restated as prose is cut.
